Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Martin Health AI is a personal tool used only by its developer, Martin Brekke, on his own Google account. It has no other users.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app reads the owner's own Google Health data: activity and fitness, health metrics and measurements (such as heart rate, blood oxygen, weight and body fat), sleep, profile and settings.

How it is used and stored

The data is used only so the owner can review and analyse their own health information. It is stored in a private, access-controlled repository that only the owner can open. The app's credentials are kept as encrypted secrets.

Sharing

The data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone else, and it is never used for advertising. It is not used to train machine-learning models.

Deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from their Google Account permissions page and can delete the stored data by deleting the private repository.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
